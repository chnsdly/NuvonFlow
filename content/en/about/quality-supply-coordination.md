## Quality coordination

Quality coordination begins by making the requested scope visible: product family, material, pressure rating, connection, quantity, inspection expectations, documents, packaging, and any customer-specific requirements. This reduces the risk of quoting one scope and delivering another.

## Supply coordination

For project orders, Nuvon can organize line items by service, material, document requirement, and shipment group. This helps align supplier confirmation, packing details, shipping documents, spare parts, and customer review.

## Practical controls

Useful controls include RFQ checklists, clear assumptions in quotations, revision-aware datasheets, drawing confirmation, inspection notes, packing requirements, label requirements, and document registers.

## After-sales communication

If a replacement, warranty question, or document correction is needed, the same structure helps: identify the original order or project reference, product family, tag or line item, installation conditions, photos if available, and the specific issue to review.
