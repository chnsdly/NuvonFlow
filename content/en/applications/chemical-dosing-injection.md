## Application context

Chemical dosing and injection systems depend on predictable pump output, compatible wetted materials, and clear accessory selection. The visible valve package may be small, but the duty can be demanding: concentrated chemicals, intermittent injection, tubing transitions, blocked discharge risk, siphoning, crystallization, or off-gassing.

For a reviewable quotation, the scope should identify whether the request is for individual valves, a pump discharge accessory set, an injection assembly, a dosing skid, replacement stock, or a project BOM.

## Typical duty conditions

| Process area | Common flow control role | Information to confirm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chemical storage and suction | Tank outlet isolation, straining, calibration, and suction piping | Chemical name, concentration, viscosity, suction lift, priming method |
| Metering pump discharge | Back pressure, relief, pulsation control, and discharge isolation | Pump capacity, discharge pressure, set pressure, tubing or pipe size |
| Injection point | Check protection, anti-siphon behavior, nozzle or quill details | Main line pressure, orientation, injection fitting, material compatibility |
| Flushing and maintenance | Isolation, drain, bypass, calibration, and spare accessory planning | Cleaning chemical, maintenance frequency, unions, spare seals |

## Selection risks to clarify

- Sodium hypochlorite, oxidizers, strong acids, caustic, polymers, and solvents may require different body, seat, seal, and diaphragm materials.
- A metering pump may need back pressure to operate consistently, especially when injecting into low-pressure or variable-pressure lines.
- Relief paths must be clear; a relief valve without a safe return or drain arrangement may not solve the overpressure risk.
- Small tubing connections, adapters, and injection fittings should be included in the scope if the project needs a usable assembly.

## RFQ preparation

Share chemical name, concentration, temperature, dosing rate, pump type, suction condition, discharge pressure, desired set pressure, injection point pressure, tubing or pipe size, end connections, required accessories, quantity, spare parts, and target documents.
