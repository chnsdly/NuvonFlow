## Application context

Chemical processing facilities often need a coordinated mix of manual valves, automated valves, check valves, fittings, pressure control items, and documentation. The same plant may include clean transfer, corrosive service, intermittent batches, flushing steps, utility chemicals, and maintenance bypasses.

The useful scope is organized by process step. Reactor feed, storage transfer, neutralization, washdown, and packaged skids can each require a different balance of material compatibility, shutoff reliability, automation, cleanability, and inspection documents.

## Typical duty conditions

| Process area | Common flow control role | Information to confirm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reactor feed and batch transfer | Isolation, sequencing, check protection, and controlled transfer | Media variation, batch frequency, pressure, temperature, cleaning method |
| Utility chemical lines | Acid, alkali, solvent, oxidizer, or cleaning chemical distribution | Chemical list, concentration, pipe standard, connection style, leakage tolerance |
| Neutralization and washdown | Chemical dosing, mixing, drain, flushing, and maintenance isolation | pH range, solids, cleaning chemicals, cycle frequency, spare parts |
| Packaged skids and project BOMs | Coordinated valves, actuators, fittings, accessories, and documents | Line list, tag numbers, drawings, submittal scope, testing expectations |

[Ball valves](/products/ball-valves/) may fit clean chemical isolation. [Diaphragm valves](/products/diaphragm-valves/) are often reviewed for corrosive or particulate service. [Check valves](/products/check-valves/) protect pumps and separate lines. [Actuators](/products/actuators/) support batch, recipe, utility, or safety sequences. [Fittings](/products/fittings/) and [pressure control and chemfeed items](/products/pressure-control-chemfeed/) help turn the request into a complete project scope.

## Selection risks to clarify

- Batch operations may expose the same valve to multiple chemicals, cleaning fluids, and temperatures.
- Automation scope should identify signals, fail position, feedback, local override, enclosure rating, and testing needs.
- Cleaning, flushing, or intermittent drying can affect elastomers, seats, diaphragms, and liners.
- Project orders should define documents, tag references, inspection notes, packaging, spare parts, and revision control before quotation.

## RFQ preparation

Clarify the process step, media list, concentration range, temperature, pressure, cleaning method, valve function, actuation requirement, pipe standard, connection, quantity, tag or drawing references, commercial project stage, and whether documents must align with a formal submittal package.
