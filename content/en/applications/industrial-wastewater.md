## Application context

Industrial wastewater service is often less predictable than clean water service. Media can change by production batch, cleaning cycle, pH adjustment, precipitation step, or discharge requirement. Lines may carry suspended solids, precipitates, biological content, oils, oxidizers, acids, alkalis, salts, or cleaning waste.

The useful discussion starts with how difficult the line is to isolate, drain, flush, and maintain. A low-cost valve that works on clean water may be the wrong choice if solids settle, scale builds, elastomers are attacked, or a pump cycles intermittently.

## Typical duty conditions

| Process area | Common flow control role | Information to confirm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Equalization and transfer | Tank isolation, pump discharge, bypass, and drain valves | Solids content, flow variability, pressure, cleaning access |
| pH adjustment and precipitation | Chemical dosing, mixing, neutralization, and slurry handling | pH range, chemical additives, precipitates, abrasion, line flushing |
| Sludge or dirty-water lines | Isolation, check protection, maintenance removal, and cleanout | Solids size, settling risk, valve orientation, unions, spare parts |
| Odor control and scrubber systems | Chemical circulation, blowdown, and low-pressure transfer | Chemical exposure, temperature, pump cycling, gasket materials |

## Product families usually reviewed

[Diaphragm valves](/products/diaphragm-valves/) are often reviewed for corrosive or dirty liquid isolation. [Butterfly valves](/products/butterfly-valves/) may fit larger low-pressure headers, while [check valves](/products/check-valves/) protect pumps and help prevent reverse flow. [Fittings](/products/fittings/) and unions are especially important when maintenance access is limited. [Actuators](/products/actuators/) may be needed for sequenced treatment steps.

## Selection risks to clarify

- Suspended solids and precipitates can affect valve closing, seat wear, and check valve behavior.
- pH swings, oxidizers, chlorides, and cleaning chemicals can change material recommendations.
- Intermittent pump operation may require review of cracking pressure, water hammer, and drain-back behavior.
- Maintenance access, removal clearance, and union placement should be discussed before the project reaches installation.

## RFQ preparation

Provide media description, pH range, suspended solids, abrasives, temperature, pressure, flow pattern, pipe material, valve function, pump information, installation orientation, flushing method, quantity, and document requirements. If the wastewater stream changes by batch or cleaning cycle, describe the worst credible exposure.
