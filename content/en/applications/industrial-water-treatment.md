## Application context

Industrial water treatment systems combine mechanical filtration, chemical conditioning, membrane protection, disinfection, cleaning steps, and treated-water distribution. A single project may include clean water lines, oxidizing chemicals, acid or alkali dosing, antiscalant injection, backwash headers, sample points, and packaged skids.

The practical selection question is not only which valve type fits the line. The review should connect water chemistry, dosing chemicals, pipe standard, automation sequence, maintenance access, and document expectations so the quoted scope is usable for purchasing and project review.

## Typical duty conditions

| Process area | Common flow control role | Information to confirm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pretreatment and filtration | Isolation, bypass, backwash, drain, and service valves | Pipe size, pressure, flow direction, suspended solids, maintenance access |
| RO/UF and membrane support | Chemical cleaning, antiscalant dosing, flushing, and permeate or concentrate lines | Chemical exposure, low leakage needs, cleaning temperature, connection standard |
| Chemical dosing points | Pump isolation, check valves, injection valves, back pressure, and relief | Chemical name, concentration, pump capacity, injection pressure, tubing or pipe size |
| Treated-water distribution | Manual or actuated isolation for clean-water headers and skids | Pressure class, actuation, end connection, documentation, spare parts |

## Selection risks to clarify

- Oxidizers, chlorine compounds, acids, alkalis, and cleaning chemicals may require different wetted materials.
- Low leakage isolation may matter around membrane systems, bypasses, and chemical injection points.
- Backwash or regeneration sequences can require automated valves, feedback signals, and repeatable cycle timing.
- Valve, fitting, gasket, fastener, and tubing materials should be reviewed together rather than as isolated line items.

## RFQ preparation

Share water chemistry, chemical additives, concentration, operating temperature, pressure, pipe standard, connection style, flow direction, valve function, automation requirements, quantity, target documents, and whether the request is for a single component, replacement stock, or a project package.
