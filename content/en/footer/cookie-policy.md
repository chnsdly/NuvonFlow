## Cookies and similar technologies

This website may use cookies, local storage, pixels, server logs, or similar technologies to operate the site, protect forms, understand usage, remember preferences, and improve performance.

Cookies are small files stored by the browser. Some are necessary for the website to work normally, while others may support analytics, security, or user experience improvements.

## Types of cookies that may be used

| Type | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Essential cookies | Support basic website operation, page delivery, form behavior, and security checks. |
| Security cookies | Help protect forms and traffic from spam, abuse, automated submissions, or suspicious activity. |
| Analytics cookies | Help understand page visits, traffic sources, device types, and content performance. |
| Preference cookies | Remember user choices such as language, region, or display preferences when enabled. |

If Cloudflare Turnstile, analytics tools, CDN security features, or form services are enabled, those tools may set cookies or process technical information according to their own service behavior.

## Managing cookies

Users can manage or delete cookies through browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect forms, security checks, language preferences, analytics accuracy, or normal website functions.

## Updates

This cookie policy may be updated as the website's analytics, form handling, security, or hosting configuration changes.
