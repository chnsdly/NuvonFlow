## Technical information

Website content is intended for preliminary product, application, and project communication. It may help users prepare RFQs and organize duty conditions, but it is not a substitute for project-specific engineering review.

Chemical compatibility, pressure-temperature limits, installation method, actuator sizing, connection standard, inspection requirements, and compliance obligations must be confirmed against actual project conditions, applicable standards, customer specifications, and approved documents.

## Material compatibility

Material guidance on this website is general and may not account for all concentration, temperature, impurity, abrasion, solvent, oxidizing, cleaning, intermittent exposure, outdoor, or mixed-service conditions. Final material selection should be confirmed by qualified project stakeholders.

## Documents and examples

Datasheet references, drawings, tables, examples, diagrams, and article content are provided for communication and RFQ preparation. They do not automatically create an approved document package for a specific order.

## No automatic approval

Receiving information from this website, submitting an RFQ, or receiving a preliminary response does not mean that a product is approved for a specific application. Final approval should be based on quotation scope, technical review, customer confirmation, and applicable project documents.

## Limitation

Nuvon aims to keep website information useful and current, but errors, omissions, outdated content, or differences between general examples and actual project requirements may occur. Users should verify critical information before purchasing, installing, or operating any product.
