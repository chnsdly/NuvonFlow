## Scope

This privacy policy explains how Nuvon Flow Control handles information submitted through this website, including contact forms, RFQ requests, document requests, email communication, and normal website usage data.

This notice is a practical privacy baseline for website inquiries. It may need to be reviewed and adapted for the final legal company name, operating jurisdiction, CRM setup, analytics tools, and form processing workflow.

## Information collected

Nuvon may collect the following information when it is submitted or generated through normal website use:

- Name, company, role, email address, phone number, country or region, and business contact details
- Product interests, application notes, duty conditions, RFQ content, file requests, and project stage
- Attachments or information later shared by email, such as BOMs, drawings, datasheets, photos, or document registers
- Communication records needed to respond to inquiries, quotations, project clarification, document requests, or after-sales questions
- Basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, referring page, pages visited, and approximate time of visit

## How information is used

Information is used to respond to inquiries, prepare quotations, organize product and material review, coordinate technical documents, improve website content, maintain business records, support security checks, and follow up on project communication.

Nuvon does not intend to sell personal information submitted through this website. If analytics, security, email, CRM, or form handling services are used, information may be processed by those service providers for the purpose of operating the website and supporting business communication.

## Sharing project information

Project information may be shared with relevant suppliers, logistics providers, technical partners, or service providers when needed to prepare a response, quotation, document package, shipment, or support activity. Sensitive commercial or technical information should be clearly marked by the sender.

## International communication

Website inquiries may involve international business communication. Information may be processed or accessed in countries or regions different from the sender's location, depending on email, hosting, CRM, analytics, and support tools used by the business.

## Data retention

Nuvon may retain inquiry and project communication records for as long as needed to respond to the request, prepare quotations, maintain business history, support warranty or after-sales communication, comply with recordkeeping needs, or resolve disputes.

## Security

Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures should be used to protect website and project information. No website, email system, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## User choices

Users may contact Nuvon to request correction, deletion, or limitation of information submitted through the website, subject to reasonable business, legal, recordkeeping, and technical constraints.
