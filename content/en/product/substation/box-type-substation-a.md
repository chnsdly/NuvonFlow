This model follows a European-style prefabricated concept that combines medium-voltage switchgear, distribution transformer, and low-voltage assemblies inside one compact enclosure. The internal arrangement can be configured in triangular or linear compartment layouts, enabling efficient deployment where installation area and civil interface complexity must be controlled.

Its base and structural frame are manufactured with channel steel for robust rigidity during transportation and long-term operation. The enclosure can be specified in non-metallic panels, stainless steel, or cold-rolled steel depending on site conditions and corrosion expectations. Inter-compartment partitions, double-layer roof and side walls, plus temperature-controlled ventilation in transformer and LV rooms help improve thermal performance, operational reliability, and maintenance accessibility.
