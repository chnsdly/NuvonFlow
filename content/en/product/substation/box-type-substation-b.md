Substation B is optimized for industrial duty where continuity of supply and maintainability are key project targets. It integrates MV incoming section, transformer step-down section, and LV outgoing section into a factory-assembled package, reducing field assembly work and lowering schedule risk compared with multi-equipment on-site integration.

The product uses a high-strength channel-steel base and skeleton to handle lifting, relocation, and long service cycles. Enclosure material can be selected according to corrosion class and appearance requirements, while compartmentalized separation and interlock philosophy reduce cross-interference during inspection. Transformer rating, wiring method, and key MV/LV component selection can be customized to match load profile and project-specific engineering standards.
