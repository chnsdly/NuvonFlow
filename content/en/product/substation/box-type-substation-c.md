This configuration is tailored for outdoor distribution points including wind farm auxiliaries, scenic-area feeders, road lighting power nodes, and municipal service infrastructure. The MV room, transformer room, and LV room are pre-integrated in one station body, so on-site work is largely limited to foundation, cable termination, and commissioning.

Its compact architecture supports easier placement in land-constrained projects and offers practical flexibility for relocation or phased expansion. A double-layer roof and side-wall design helps limit solar heat impact, while automatic temperature-controlled ventilation improves internal cooling conditions. Enclosure materials can be selected for coastal humidity, dusty environments, or standard urban exposure, balancing environmental protection, appearance quality, and routine inspection convenience.
