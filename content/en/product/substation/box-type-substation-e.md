This model targets building distribution applications in residential communities, mixed-use complexes, high-rise developments, and public service facilities. MV room, transformer room, and LV room are factory-integrated and tested before delivery, improving on-site installation consistency and reducing dependence on conventional indoor substation construction schedules.

In project execution, customers can specify transformer capacity classes, metering or compensation units, and the number of MV/LV outgoing circuits. The enclosure is available in non-metallic, stainless steel, or cold-rolled steel variants to align environmental durability with façade requirements. With its compact footprint and maintainable internal layout, the series supports reliable power distribution in dense urban areas while keeping lifecycle service efficiency manageable.
