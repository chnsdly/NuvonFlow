GCK cabinets are designed for projects requiring compact layout and high functional integration in low-voltage switch rooms. Standardized withdrawable units of different sizes can be freely combined, enabling tailored circuit planning while maintaining lineup consistency.

The platform features insulated vertical busbar and outgoing connection protection, dedicated mechanical locking with clear operating-state indication, and smooth drawer movement mechanism with good interchangeability. These attributes support safer operation, easier maintenance, and efficient expansion in power-intensive facilities.
