GGJ series adopts mature domestic compensation technology and selected low-voltage components to provide centralized reactive power management. By reducing unnecessary reactive current circulation, the cabinet helps release transformer and feeder capacity while lowering distribution losses.

In addition to automatic compensation control, the unit can monitor key grid parameters such as three-phase voltage, current, and power factor in real time. This enables users in manufacturing, metallurgy, chemical processing, hospitals, and transport hubs to balance energy efficiency objectives with operational visibility and compliance requirements.
