Designed for low-voltage distribution applications, GGJ cabinets support staged or automatic reactive power compensation according to real-time load variation. This approach is beneficial for sites with fluctuating inductive loads, where stable power factor contributes to improved system economy and voltage performance.

The platform is commonly used in industrial plants, commercial complexes, petrochemical facilities, and public infrastructure. By integrating compensation and measurement in one lineup, users gain both corrective capability and data reference for ongoing electrical optimization and maintenance planning.
