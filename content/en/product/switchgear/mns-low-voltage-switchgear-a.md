MNS is a new-generation low-voltage withdrawable platform developed from mainstream domestic and international switchgear technology routes. The cabinet framework, drawer units, and busbar channel architecture are optimized for high assembly efficiency and dependable electrical continuity across incoming, feeder, motor control, and compensation sections.

Because fixed and withdrawable schemes can share a common busbar system, projects can deploy hybrid lineups that balance investment, maintainability, and operational flexibility. The enclosed functional busbar channel and reinforced protection design improve personnel safety, while front-access drawer operation and cable maintenance help reduce outage time during routine service and expansion work.
