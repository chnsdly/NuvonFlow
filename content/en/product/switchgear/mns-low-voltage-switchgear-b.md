The MNS platform is engineered for medium and large low-voltage distribution projects that require high circuit density in limited cabinet footprint. Standardized PC and MCC sections can be arranged with reactive compensation and soft-start branches to simplify system integration and future expansion.

Its mixed fixed-withdrawable assembly strategy enables users to allocate critical feeders and frequent-operation circuits according to maintenance priorities. With robust mechanical interlocking, protected functional compartments, and convenient cable access, the cabinet supports safer operation and more predictable maintenance management across the project lifecycle.
