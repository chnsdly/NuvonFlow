Distribution cabinets in this class are used to route and protect electrical energy through multiple panel levels, combining breakers, fuses, indicators, meters, and auxiliary control devices according to project design philosophy. They are fundamental assets in industrial, commercial, and public utility power rooms.

Engineering value depends not only on component selection but also on execution details such as proper cabinet framing, compliant PE conductor arrangement, tidy branch wiring, complete hinge/lock hardware, and qualified enclosure anti-corrosion treatment. Consistent quality control improves reliability during commissioning and operation.
