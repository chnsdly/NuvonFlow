The cabinet system connects upstream switchgear or transformer outputs with downstream distribution boards, delivering coordinated protection and operational control for motors, lighting, and process loads. Its architecture allows flexible adaptation to different circuit counts and functional zoning requirements.

Field reliability is strengthened by disciplined installation and acceptance practices, including verticality correction, terminal tightening, cable organization, grounding continuity checks, and gap sealing around cabinet structures. These quality measures directly affect fault rate, safety margin, and maintenance workload.
