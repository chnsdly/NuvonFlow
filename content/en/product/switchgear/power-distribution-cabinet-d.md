As core assets in electrical distribution infrastructure, these cabinets integrate multiple apparatus types into a standardized enclosure for organized power management. They are commonly deployed between utility-side equipment and terminal sub-distribution panels in factories, campuses, and large buildings.

To achieve expected service life and safety level, projects should enforce technical controls over mounting precision, grounding conductor capacity, component fixation, panel wiring arrangement, and anti-rust coating on enclosure weld points. Proper commissioning and periodic inspection complete the reliability management loop.
