Nuvon organizes product information by how the component is usually selected in a corrosion-resistant flow control project. Use these pages to compare valve function, material family, pressure and temperature considerations, actuation options, connection style, and the documents typically requested for quotation or submittal.

For exact sizing, pressure rating, chemical compatibility, and trim selection, the final review should be based on the actual service conditions and project documents.

## Product direction by duty

| Starting condition | Product direction to review | RFQ notes that matter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clean chemical isolation, skid shutoff, sample points | Ball valves | Body, seat, seal, connection, manual or actuated operation |
| Larger water, wastewater, and utility lines | Butterfly valves | Liner, disc, shaft, flange standard, pressure class, actuator torque |
| Corrosive, abrasive, slurry-like, or off-gassing service | Diaphragm valves | Body or lining, diaphragm compound, vacuum, cycle frequency, solids |
| Pump discharge, dosing lines, and backflow prevention | Check valves | Cracking pressure, flow direction, orientation, pressure drop, solids |
| Metering pump accessories and injection assemblies | Pressure control and chemfeed | Pump data, back pressure, relief setting, calibration and injection point |
| Automated valve packages | Actuators | Torque, safety factor, fail position, power or air supply, controls |
| Pipe transitions, unions, strainers, adapters, and spares | Fittings | Material, gasket, fastener, end standard, pressure rating, quantity |
