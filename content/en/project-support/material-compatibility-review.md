## What compatibility review covers

Material compatibility is a structured review, not a single yes-or-no answer. The same chemical can behave differently depending on concentration, temperature, pressure, aeration, contaminants, exposure time, intermittent drying, and cleaning practice.

For flow control products, the weak point is not always the valve body. Seats, seals, diaphragms, liners, gaskets, fasteners, tubing, adapters, and instrument accessories may all need attention.

## Typical questions

| Question | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| What is the exact media and concentration? | A broad chemical family can include very different corrosion behavior. |
| What is the temperature range? | Compatibility may change sharply at elevated temperature or during cleaning. |
| Is the service oxidizing, abrasive, crystallizing, or solvent-based? | The risk may shift from body corrosion to seat, seal, diaphragm, or wear issues. |
| Is exposure continuous or intermittent? | Drying, crystallization, flushing, and startup conditions can affect the recommendation. |
| Are there outdoor or corrosive atmosphere conditions? | External fasteners, actuator housings, coatings, and accessories may need review. |

## Information to prepare

Provide media name, concentration, impurities, temperature, pressure, flow pattern, solids, cleaning chemicals, exposure duration, pipe material, installation environment, and any project standard or customer-approved material list.
