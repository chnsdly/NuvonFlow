## Why duty conditions matter

Most selection delays begin with missing operating information. A valve name alone is not enough for corrosive or chemical service because body material, seat material, elastomer, actuator size, end connection, pressure class, and document package all depend on the actual duty.

Clear duty conditions help the first response move from broad guessing to useful clarification: which product families fit, which assumptions are risky, which documents are needed, and which items require customer or engineering confirmation.

## What to send first

| Information group | Useful details |
| --- | --- |
| Media and chemistry | Chemical name, concentration, pH range, solids, oxidizers, solvents, cleaning fluids, and mixed exposure |
| Operating limits | Normal and design temperature, normal and design pressure, flow rate, pump capacity, cycle frequency, vacuum or pressure surge |
| Piping and installation | Pipe size, connection standard, orientation, available space, outdoor exposure, maintenance access, and line material |
| Product function | Isolation, throttling, check protection, dosing, pressure relief, back pressure, automation, or flushing |
| Project scope | Quantity, tag numbers, destination, project stage, packaging, spare parts, required documents, and target delivery |

## How Nuvon uses the information

Nuvon uses the submitted conditions to narrow product families, prepare material and accessory questions, identify missing data, and build a quotation scope that can be reviewed line by line. If the request is incomplete, the response can still be useful by listing assumptions and clarification questions instead of burying uncertainty inside a price line.

## What still needs confirmation

Duty condition review is an initial commercial and technical organization step. Final selection should still confirm material compatibility, pressure-temperature limits, actuator sizing, connection standards, inspection requirements, and any customer-specific project specifications.

The fastest starting point is to send the known duty conditions through the [request a quote](/request-a-quote/) form, even if some fields are still uncertain.
