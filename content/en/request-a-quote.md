## What to include

For a faster first review, include the operating medium, concentration, temperature, working pressure, pipe size, end connection, valve function, actuation requirement, quantity, destination country, and any document requirements.

If the service is abrasive, oxidizing, high cycle, vacuum, outdoor, intermittent, crystallizing, solvent-based, or cleaned with a different chemical, mention that as well. These details often affect body material, seat and seal choice, actuator sizing, check valve behavior, and accessory scope.

## Helpful attachments

Useful follow-up files include BOMs, line lists, P&ID snippets, drawings, existing product photos, pump data, document registers, inspection requirements, and customer-approved material lists. If the file package is large, send the first message through the form and coordinate attachments by email after Nuvon replies.

## What happens next

Nuvon may respond with clarification questions, a suggested product family, a material review direction, a basic quotation structure, or a checklist for documents needed before a formal submittal package is prepared.

Submitting an RFQ does not automatically approve a product for the application. Final scope should be confirmed through quotation, technical review, customer confirmation, and applicable project documents.
