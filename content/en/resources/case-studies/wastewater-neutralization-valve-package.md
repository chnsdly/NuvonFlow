A wastewater neutralization project may begin with a short request such as “plastic valves for acid and alkali lines.” That description identifies the general environment, but it is not enough to review valve construction or prepare a controlled quotation.

This representative case shows how the request can be made reviewable. It does not describe a named customer project or a completed supply reference.

## Initial request

The first list included isolation valves, check valves, and several actuated valves. It did not distinguish chemical unloading, storage transfer, dosing, recirculation, flushing, and treated-water duties.

The missing information created several uncertainties:

- Chemical names, concentrations, and possible mixed exposure
- Normal and design pressure and temperature
- Solids, crystallization, cleaning, and intermittent drying
- Pipe size, end standard, installation orientation, and available actuator space
- Required drawings, data sheets, manuals, declarations, and inspection records

## Review structure

The valve list was reorganized by process area and line function. Each line item then received fields for media, concentration, pressure-temperature limits, size, connection, quantity, operating mode, and document requirements.

| Process area | Typical valve function | Review focus |
|---|---|---|
| Chemical transfer | Isolation and backflow protection | Wetted materials, seals, connection standard |
| Dosing and recirculation | Isolation, check, and control support | Low-flow behavior, cycling, crystallization |
| Neutralization tank | Inlet, outlet, drain, and sampling | Solids, accessibility, cleaning method |
| Utility and flushing | Isolation and maintenance support | Cross-service exposure and connection consistency |

## Resulting RFQ scope

The revised RFQ separated confirmed requirements from open questions. Product families could then be reviewed without treating every line as identical, while quotations could state assumptions instead of silently filling information gaps.

The next step would be to confirm each proposed material, pressure-temperature boundary, actuator, and document set against the selected product series.
