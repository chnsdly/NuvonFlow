KYN28 Armored Removable AC Metal-Enclosed Switchgear C

This KYN28 configuration targets 3.6–12kV switching and protection duties with withdrawable breaker units, compartmentalized construction, and five-prevention interlock logic to improve personnel safety and operating reliability in medium-voltage installations.

Request a Quote

Product Details

The cabinet framework uses armored metal-enclosed construction to separate busbar, breaker, cable, and low-voltage control sections, supporting safer maintenance practices and reduced disturbance between functional zones. It is suited for projects that require clear medium-voltage operation procedures and robust service continuity.

Core primary components generally include a high-voltage circuit breaker, earthing switch, and current/voltage transformers. Combined with complete interlocking strategy, the product addresses frequent operational risks in medium-voltage yards and conforms to prevailing domestic design standards for this class of switchgear.
